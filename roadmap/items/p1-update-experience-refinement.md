# P1 Update Experience Refinement

## Summary
Reduce friction and ambiguity in update discovery and installation flow.

## Scope
- Review and refine inline update notice states.
- Improve install-flow messaging and user guidance.

## Out of Scope
- Full redesign of release delivery infrastructure.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Update states are clear and actionable.
- Messaging explains success, failure, and next steps.

## Test Plan
- Validate notice behavior for update available, no update, and update check error states.
- Validate install flow messaging across expected outcomes.

## Risks and Dependencies
- Depends on update check service behavior and installer handoff.

## Notes
- Roadmap target: v1.2.11.
